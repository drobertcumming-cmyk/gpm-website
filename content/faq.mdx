# Frequently asked questions.

Organized by topic. If your question is not here, the advisor call is available — the call does not commit you to anything, and the advisor is salaried.

## Pricing

**What is Grace's spread?** Our spread on gold is 11.1%, all-in. That is the full markup over spot. No admin fee, no setup fee, no second-layer charges.

**Is the 11.1% negotiable?** No. The spread is constant. It does not vary by customer, purchase amount, negotiation, or advisor relationship.

**Is there a minimum rollover to access the published spread?** No. The 11.1% applies to the first dollar of your rollover and to the last.

**What does the buyback look like?** We buy back at the spot price on the day of the transaction, sourced from a published third-party spot price. No markdown. No "liquidation fee." No buyback spread.

**Are there fees beyond Grace's spread?** Two costs are not Grace's and are paid by every Gold IRA customer regardless of dealer: the IRS-approved custodian's annual fee and the IRS-approved depository's annual storage fee. Your custodian and depository disclose these directly.

**What happens if Grace changes its spread in future?** Any change to the published spread would be a CFO-signed-off policy update, with the new figure and effective date displayed on the pricing page. Existing customer accounts would be governed by the terms in place at the time of their purchase.

## Rollovers and transfers

**What accounts can be rolled over?** 401(k), 403(b), traditional IRA, Roth IRA, SEP IRA, and most other qualified retirement accounts. Current-employer 401(k)s generally cannot be rolled over while you are still employed by the sponsoring employer, unless you are over 59½ and the plan permits an in-service rollover.

**Is a rollover a taxable event?** A properly executed trustee-to-trustee transfer or direct rollover is not a taxable event. The tax-deferred (or tax-free, for Roth) status of the original account is preserved.

**How long does a rollover take?** Typically ten to fifteen business days from initiation to completion. Timing depends on how quickly your current plan administrator releases funds.

**What if I already hold a Gold IRA and want to transfer?** The first step is requesting a written buyback quote from your current provider — that number tells you what the round-trip cost of your original purchase has been. The transfer mechanics are on our transfer guide.

**Can I roll over only a portion of my retirement account?** Yes. Most customers do. The appropriate allocation is a decision for you in consultation with qualified professionals.

## Custodians and depositories

**Does Grace hold the metals?** No. Metals in a Gold IRA are held by an IRS-approved depository in your name, administered by an IRS-approved custodian. Grace is the dealer — we sell you the metal and buy it back when you sell. The custodian and depository are independent parties.

**Which custodians and depositories does Grace work with?** Specific partner names will be listed on the compliance page once our partnership agreements are finalized. Your advisor can walk you through the options available to you.

**Can I choose my own custodian?** The Gold IRA structure requires an IRS-approved custodian. We work with a defined set of approved custodians and will discuss the options available to you.

**What happens to my metals if Grace goes out of business?** Your metals are held by the depository in your name, administered by the custodian. The three-party structure is designed to protect your holdings from the commercial risk of any single party. Your metals remain yours regardless of Grace's commercial status.

## Products

**What do you sell?** Standard IRS-eligible bullion — American Eagles, Canadian Maple Leafs, LBMA-approved gold bars, and similar standard bullion products.

**Do you sell numismatic, proof, or premium coins?** No. We do not sell numismatic, rare, exclusive, proof, premium, limited-release, or similar-labeled coins in any form. This is a published, permanent policy.

**Why not?** The full answer is on our numismatic coins pillar. The short answer is that premium coin sales in Gold IRAs are the most documented source of consumer harm in the category, and we are structurally not in that business.

**What about silver, platinum, palladium?** At launch, Grace's catalogue is gold. Expansion to other IRS-eligible metals may follow based on customer demand. Any expansion would apply the same pricing discipline: a published spread, buyback at spot, standard bullion only.

## Tax and regulatory

**Is Grace a financial advisor?** No. Grace Precious Metals is a precious metals dealer. We are not a financial, tax, or legal advisor.

**Do you provide tax advice?** No. We can explain the general tax treatment of IRA rollovers and distributions, but we are not a tax advisor and we do not file your taxes. Consult a qualified tax professional for advice on your specific circumstances.

**Is Grace state-licensed?** Yes, in jurisdictions where state dealer licensing is required. The state-by-state licensing detail is on the compliance page.

**Is Grace regulated by the SEC?** The SEC regulates securities, not physical precious metals. The CFTC has jurisdiction over precious metals fraud, and the FTC applies substantiation standards to advertising. We operate under both frameworks and under applicable state regulation.
