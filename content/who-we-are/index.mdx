# Built by a pastor, an accountant, and a marketing executive who knew the industry from the inside.

William Armour is a pastor who spent a decade in the Gold IRA industry before his pastoral conviction required him to leave. Andrew Armour, William's brother, is a qualified accountant — the person who signs off on every pricing figure on this website. Duncan Cumming worked inside the category before co-founding Grace and brought to the company a working knowledge of how the industry markets itself — including the parts of that marketing Grace is deliberately built to refuse.

Grace Precious Metals is the company the three of them built. It is a Gold IRA company organised around a single architectural commitment: the price is on the page before the phone rings. Everything else — the salaried advisors, the bullion-only catalogue, the buyback at spot, the published revenue model — is a structural expression of that commitment, not a marketing position around it.

## The founding principle

*"A false balance is an abomination to the Lord, but a just weight is his delight."*

— Proverbs 11:1

The verse is not decorative. It is the operational principle the business is organised around. The just weight is what the customer is charged. Ours is visible — the number is published. Accurate — what is published is what is charged. Constant — it does not move between customers.

Every structural decision at Grace resolves to one of those three attributes. The published spread is the visible weight. The salaried-advisor model protects the accuracy of the weight, by removing the financial incentive that historically pulls it out of true. The bullion-only catalogue protects the constancy of the weight, by removing the product line that in this category is most often used to vary the price for different customers. The buyback at spot completes the commitment on the exit side, so that the weight the customer was charged on entry is the weight they are paid on exit, without a second margin hidden in the transaction.

We chose Proverbs 11:1 as the anchor because it is the most exact description of what the category, at its worst, has failed to do — and of what we are structurally committed to doing differently.

## The commitments

Four foundational commitments. Each one operational, each one verifiable.

**Published spread, 11.1% all-in.** Full pricing detail and worked examples on the pricing page. [Our pricing →](/pricing)

**Buyback at spot, never below.** When you sell back to us, we pay the spot price on the day. No markdown, no "liquidation fee," no second margin on exit.

**Standard IRS-eligible bullion only.** American Eagles, Canadian Maple Leafs, LBMA-approved bars. No numismatic, "exclusive," "rare," "premium," or "proof" coins in any form. Published, permanent policy.

**Salaried advisors, no commission.** The person you speak to is paid the same whether you open an account or not. No commissions, no referral fees, no volume bonuses.

## What we are not

We are not a financial advisor. We are not a tax advisor. We are not a legal advisor. We are a precious metals dealer. We sell gold, we hold it on your behalf through an IRS-approved custodian and depository, and we buy it back from you at spot when you choose to sell. Whether gold belongs in your retirement account at all, and in what proportion, is a decision you should make with qualified professionals who can see your full financial picture. We do not attempt to make that decision for you, and we do not structure our advisors' incentives in a way that would encourage them to try.
