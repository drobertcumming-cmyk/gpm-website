# What you should be able to evaluate before you choose a Gold IRA company.

The Resources library is where we publish longer-form guides on the questions our customers actually ask — and on the questions most of the industry prefers you do not ask. At launch, three pillars: how buyback really works, how to read round-trip cost, and why we refuse to sell numismatic coins. These are not sales pages. They are the evaluation framework. We invite you to use it — on Grace and on anyone else you are considering.

Each pillar is a comprehensive treatment of its topic, supported by two shorter pieces that go deeper on specific aspects. The library will grow over the next twelve months with clusters covering salaried-vs-commissioned advisor compensation, inherited IRA edge cases, custodian selection, IRS-eligible bullion specifications, and depository storage.
