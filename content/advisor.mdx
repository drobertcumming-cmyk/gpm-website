# Most Gold IRA companies were built for the transaction. We were built for the relationship.

A retirement savings decision is not a single sale. It is a relationship that lasts the length of the account — through rollover, through market cycles, through eventual sale-back, and sometimes through the passage of the account to a spouse or heirs. Grace was built for the full span of that relationship.

The advisor call is a thirty-minute conversation with a member of our team. It is not a sales call. It is a structured conversation designed to help you understand whether a Gold IRA is the right decision for your circumstances, and if it is, what the mechanics of your specific rollover or transfer would look like.

The advisor you speak to is salaried. They earn the same compensation whether you open an account or not. There is no commission, no referral fee, no volume bonus tied to the outcome of the call. What that means, practically, is that the conversation is structured around your situation rather than around a target the advisor needs to hit.

## What to expect from the call

**Before the call.** You book a time that works for your schedule. Your advisor receives a brief note with whatever context you have chosen to share — typically your rough rollover amount, the account type you are moving from, and any specific questions you want covered. The call is not gated on providing any of that information; you can book a call having told us only your name.

**On the call.** The advisor starts by asking what you are trying to solve. Not what product you want to buy — what question you are trying to answer. The conversation covers your circumstances, your timeline, the rollover or transfer mechanics that apply to your situation, the specific custodian and depository options available to you, and the cost structure worked out against your actual amount. The advisor answers questions. They do not ask you to decide on the call. They do not introduce urgency into the call that is not actually present.

**After the call.** The advisor sends a follow-up email with a summary of what was discussed, any worked examples calculated against your specific amount, and the next steps if you want to proceed. If you do not want to proceed, no further contact. If you want to think about it, the advisor does not follow up with a "limited time" offer, because there isn't one. When you are ready, you are ready. If you are not ready, or if Grace is not the right fit, that is a legitimate outcome of the call.

## Common questions

**Is the call really free?** Yes. No charge, no obligation to proceed.

**Will I be added to a mailing list if I book?** Only if you opt in during the booking. The default is no.

**What if I want to do this without a call at all?** You can. The pricing, worked examples, rollover mechanics, and transfer guide are on the website in full. The advisor call is available if you want it. It is not required.

**What if I book a call and then change my mind?** Cancel or reschedule through the confirmation email. No penalty, no follow-up pressure.

**How are advisors compensated?** Salary. There is no commission, no referral fee, and no volume bonus. The advisor's income does not vary based on the outcome of your call.
