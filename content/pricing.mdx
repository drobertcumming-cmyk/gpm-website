## Why the number is on this page

Most of the industry will not show you a number until you are on a call with a commissioned salesperson. We think that is the wrong order. A retirement account is not something you should have to negotiate from a position of incomplete information, against a person paid more when you buy more.

Proverbs 11:1 says *a false balance is an abomination to the Lord, but a just weight is his delight.* The weight is what you are charged. Ours is visible, accurate, and constant. Visible, because the number is on this page. Accurate, because what is published is what is charged. Constant, because it does not move between customers. That is what we mean when we say we publish the number.

## The four foundational commitments

Our pricing posture is not a promotion. It is four foundational commitments, each one operational and verifiable.

**1. Published spread: 11.1%, all-in.** The markup over spot is 11.1%. That is the whole markup. No second layer labelled "administration," no third layer labelled "setup." The number you see is the number you pay.

**2. Buyback at spot, never below.** When you sell your metals back to us, we pay the spot price on the day. We do not mark the buyback down to create a second margin on exit. The exit number is the market number.

**3. Standard IRS-eligible bullion only.** We sell American Eagles, Canadian Maple Leafs, LBMA-approved bars, and other standard IRS-eligible bullion. We do not sell numismatic, "exclusive," "rare," "premium," or "proof" coins in any form. This is a published, permanent policy. It is also the single most important piece of consumer protection in this category — category-level enforcement records document hundreds of millions of dollars in consumer losses tied to numismatic coin pricing. None of it happens here, because none of it is on the catalogue.

**4. Salaried advisors. No commission.** The advisor you speak to is paid the same whether you open an account or not. There are no commissions, no referral fees, no volume bonuses paid to the person on the call. What that means, practically, is that the person helping you has no financial interest in talking you into a larger transaction, a faster decision, or a product with a higher margin. The incentive and the counsel are aligned.

## How we get paid

This is the part of the category most readers have never seen written down. So here it is plainly.

Grace earns the 11.1% spread. That is our revenue on a gold purchase. Out of that margin we pay for the metal, the logistics of getting it into an IRS-approved depository, the custodian relationship, our advisors' salaries, our overhead, and our compliance. When you sell back to us, we take no markup on the buyback — you receive spot. Our economics are the entry spread. Nothing else.

We do not receive referral fees from custodians or depositories. We do not receive volume rebates for steering you toward particular products. We do not receive incremental margin on any product in the catalogue beyond the published spread. If the economics of the business require raising the spread at some future point, that would be a published policy change with the same transparency as the current number — a CFO-signed-off update to this page, not a hidden adjustment on a phone call. No such change is planned, and the current spread is what you are quoted.

That is the whole revenue model. We thought you deserved to see it.

## What we don't charge

The category's pricing complexity hides in fees that sit above or beside the spread. Ours don't exist, so we can list them plainly.

There is no administration fee. There is no setup fee. There is no account-opening fee payable to Grace. There is no annual fee payable to Grace. There is no buyback-markup — we buy at spot, never below. There is no "liquidation fee." There is no minimum purchase required to access the published spread — the 11.1% applies to the first dollar of your rollover and to the last.

There are two costs that are not ours and that every Gold IRA customer pays regardless of which company they use: the IRS-approved custodian's annual fee (typically $80–$225 depending on the custodian, disclosed in your custodian's fee schedule) and the IRS-approved depository's annual storage fee (typically 0.5%–1.0% of holdings, depending on the depository and whether storage is segregated or non-segregated). These go to the custodian and the depository, not to Grace. We do not mark them up. We do not receive a rebate on them. They are what they are, and your custodian and depository will show you the figures directly.

## Common questions about our pricing

**Is the 11.1% negotiable?** No. The spread is constant. Your salaried advisor has no authority to discount it, and no commission to protect by holding it. The first customer pays 11.1%. The thousandth customer pays 11.1%.

**Is there a minimum rollover to access the published spread?** No. The 11.1% applies to the first dollar and to the last. We do not gate published pricing behind a minimum purchase.

**What do I actually pay on exit?** Nothing to Grace beyond the buyback transaction itself, which is executed at spot. If you sell a hundred ounces back to us on a day when spot is $2,600 per ounce, you receive $260,000. There is no percentage markdown. There is no "liquidation fee." The depository may charge a final-year storage fee and the custodian may charge a closing fee — both go to those parties, not to Grace, and both are disclosed by them.

**What if the spread changes in future?** Any change to the published spread would be a published policy change, CFO-signed-off, reflected on this page with the new figure and the effective date. Existing customer accounts are governed by the terms in place at the time of their purchase. We would not apply a new spread retroactively.

**How do I verify the 11.1% is actually what I will be charged?** Three ways. First, the spread is on this page, CFO-signed-off, with change-management logged. Second, when your purchase confirmation arrives, the math is shown line-by-line — the spot price used, the 11.1% spread applied, the total. Third, you can compare our per-ounce price to the spot price on any independent spot source you prefer.

**Can you give me a quote without my contact information?** Yes. The worked examples above are the quote structure. If you want a rollover number calculated against your specific amount and timing, the advisor call is available — and the call does not require a commitment or a minimum to proceed.
