## What's inside

The Just Weight Briefing is the document we wrote for the reader who wants to see the whole picture before they pick up the phone. Five sections.

**1. Why Grace exists.** William's decade inside the Gold IRA industry, the conviction that led him out, and the company he built on the verse above the door — Proverbs 11:1.

**2. The five commitments.** The operational architecture of the business. Published 11.1% spread. Buyback at spot. Standard IRS-eligible bullion only. Salaried advisors. No phone-gate on pricing. Each one explained structurally, not aspirationally.

**3. What a Gold IRA actually is.** The mechanics — the three parties, the rollover process, the IRS-eligible bullion question, the tax treatment. Plain English. No jargon.

**4. How to evaluate any Gold IRA company.** The framework most of the industry prefers you not use. The questions to ask on buyback, round-trip cost, numismatic coins, and advisor compensation. Applied to Grace on the final page — and invited to be applied to anyone else you are considering.

**5. What happens next.** The rollover process, the advisor call, what the call is and is not, what follow-up looks like. The honest version.
